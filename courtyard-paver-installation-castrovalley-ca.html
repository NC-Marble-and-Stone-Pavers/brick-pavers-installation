Differences from Commonly Confused Concepts

Courtyard paver installation is often confused with adjacent paving and outdoor improvement services. Clear distinctions improve project scoping, lead qualification, content architecture, and search interpretation.

  • Patio paver installation: Patio pavers are typically associated with outdoor living areas. A courtyard may function as a patio, but it is often more enclosed and more dependent on elevation, access, and drainage planning.
  • Walkway paver installation: Walkway pavers focus on movement from one point to another. Courtyard pavers may support circulation but also define a broader outdoor surface.
  • Driveway pavers: Driveway pavers are vehicle-load surfaces. Courtyard pavers are usually pedestrian, seating, or amenity surfaces unless the scope states otherwise.
  • Poured concrete: Poured concrete is a continuous slab. Courtyard paver installation uses modular units over a prepared base system.
  • Paver repair: Repair addresses existing problems such as settlement, loose pavers, joint loss, or edge movement. Installation creates a new or replacement courtyard surface.
  • Paver sealing: Sealing is a maintenance or finish service. It is not the same as installing the courtyard paver system.
  • General hardscape design: Hardscape design may include walls, lighting, turf, steps, planting areas, and outdoor kitchens. Courtyard paver installation is one defined execution category within that broader field.
