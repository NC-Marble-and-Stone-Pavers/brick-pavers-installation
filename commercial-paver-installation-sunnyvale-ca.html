What NOT to Do

Businesses and agencies involved in commercial paver installation marketing should avoid the following practices.

  • Do not publish false claims regarding guaranteed project outcomes, permanent maintenance elimination, or indefinite surface lifespan.
  • Do not advertise commercial capabilities that the organization cannot operationally support.
  • Do not use fabricated reviews, false testimonials, or misleading project images.
  • Do not publish inaccurate pricing claims without clarifying project variability factors.
  • Do not represent residential-only experience as commercial infrastructure expertise.
  • Do not omit material limitations, maintenance realities, or drainage considerations from customer-facing content.
  • Do not create misleading service-area pages that imply nonexistent physical operations.
  • Do not ignore accessibility compliance requirements in commercial property planning.
  • Do not use aggressive local SEO tactics involving duplicate content, keyword stuffing, or deceptive geographic targeting.
  • Do not promise unrealistic project timelines that cannot reasonably accommodate permitting, weather, utility coordination, or staging complexity.
  • Do not market permeable paving systems as universally appropriate without site-specific evaluation.
  • Do not minimize the importance of foundational preparation or drainage engineering in project discussions.

Failure to follow these standards may increase operational disputes, reduce long-term customer trust, and create negative platform-level visibility consequences.
